NYAYABANDHU WINDOWS REVIEW PACKAGE

INSTALL
1. In File Explorer, right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted NyayaBandhu folder and double-click install.bat.
3. Choose an installation folder. Keep an internet connection available for
   Python and package installation.
4. Use the NyayaBandhu desktop shortcut or start.bat to open the review app.
   Use start-fresh.bat if you want a separate empty recording workspace.
   Acts and reviews in start.bat persist when you close and reopen the app.
   Re-running install.bat updates the app without clearing that workspace.

OPEN THE WORKSPACE
The reviewer workspace is at http://127.0.0.1:8000/admin.
No Acts are added to a new workspace automatically. Choose Add Act, select a
base XML and its matching source PDF, then review and approve the base text.
Upload each amendment with its matching XML and PDF in effective-date order.
The Upload amendment menu lists only prepared amendments for the base Act you
have open. Selecting one also selects its matching gazette PDF. Use the upload
fields for a new amendment that is not listed.
To remove an imported Act, choose Delete on the Acts page or Delete Act inside
the Act. To remove an amendment, choose Delete on its timeline entry. Deleted
items can be restored from Deleted Acts or Deleted amendments. An amendment
used by a saved version cannot be deleted while that version depends on it.

REVIEW POINTS
The Teachers 2022 amendment replaces two provisos with one. Inspect the
proposed change against the Gazette and the preceding version. The 2026
amendment must be reviewed against the version after the 2022 amendment.
The Kannada 2024 amendment requires its commencement notification and date.
The software's test approvals and dates are technical fixtures, not legal
approval. The reviewer must approve the actual text and dates in the app.
